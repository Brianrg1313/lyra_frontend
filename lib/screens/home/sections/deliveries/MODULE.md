# Module: deliveries section

**Path** `lib/screens/home/sections/deliveries`
**Role** Content of the `deliveries` home section (`/home/deliveries`, top side-bar group, below messages): the map fills the sheet with every branch pinned and the open map's areas drawn (per branch, the other branches' in force grey under them); over its left side the panel (title, subtitle, a search over the view on screen, the branch picked — "All branches" or one —, and two views: the branch worked in's deliveries board — the shipments, opened first, their destinations pinned on the map — and where the company delivers: the branch picked's map — "All branches" the shared one, a branch its own, picking it puts that scope in force —, "New delivery method" first, its areas and fixed/flexible, or the editor of the one being shaped), "New delivery" over its top right. "New delivery method" turns the panel into its three steps (kind → the method's form → the areas, on the map beside it). No sub-pages.
**Services** `BusinessLocationService` (`lib/services/business`), `MapTilesService` (`lib/services/maps`), `BusinessBranchesService`, `DeliveryAreasService`, `BusinessDeliveryService`, `ShipmentsService` (`lib/services/shipments`) — created and provided by `_deliveries.dart`, with `BranchesFocus`.
**Backend** `GET /business/location/` (map center), `GET /business/branches/` (pins, per-branch maps), `/business/delivery/{,options,providers}/` (the methods, pickup, note, `MEETING_POINT`; `DELIVERY_SCOPE`), `/business/delivery/areas/` (areas, `BRANCH`); in `hybrid` the settings and areas are the master's (`delivery.*`, `DeliveryLanSource`), `POST /scorpius/v1/isochrone` (travel-time outline), `/scorpius/v1` (tilesets, server-drawn raster tiles), `/shipments/{,states,carriers,windows,offer,settings}/` (the board, cloud only: the `branch_id` header's branch; `shipment/changed`), `/appointments/{hours,closures}/` (the branch's week, from the schedule panel).
**Reuses** `BranchMarkers`, `BranchGlyph`, `BranchesFocus` from `business/branches/`.
**Packages** `flutter_map` + `latlong2` (raster `TileLayer`, light/dark by the app's brightness).

## Conventions

- **`delivers`/`pickup` are three-state**: `null` is "has not said" and never "no", so pickup offers three
  answers and not a switch; a new method sets `delivers` to yes only when it was `false`. A company with options that never said reads `true` (the backend measures it).
- **Every method is local**: the form saves `COVERAGE` local and never a radius or zones, so the drawn areas alone say where
  it delivers (the backend answers a local option's radius for a point outside them). An own delivery's reach (`REACH`, shaped in its form) decides that method alone on the backend (`basis: "outline"`); the drawn areas stay the map every other method is decided with.
- **`meeting` (flexible areas) is two-state and off by default**, unlike `delivers`/`pickup`: the backend refuses
  it empty. It is company-wide, not per option, and only works with drawn areas (the backend picks the spot inside them).
- **An area is saved as geometry, never as a route**: a travel-time area is the isochrone's outline the company
  confirmed, stored as a polygon (the backend decides with `Geometry`, and routing results are not stored).
- `AreaEditor`'s ceilings are the backend's own figures (`DeliveryAreas` in lyra): radius 0.01–1000 km, 3–250 points,
  20 areas per company. Narrowing one here refuses a shape the server would have taken.
- An area's colour is its index in `chart.series` within the open map's areas (before the search filters them): the list, the probe and the map must call `areaColor` with the same index.
- **Both maps are kept**: the shared areas (`branch` null) and each branch's live side by side; `scope` decides which are in force (`inForce`), and nothing here deletes the other set. The ceiling (20) is per map, as the backend's.
- **The board is the branch worked in's** (`branch_id`; the backend reads no other and none for "all"): the branch field
  filters the areas freely, but on the board it works in the branch picked, and "All branches" shows no list.
- `deliveringBranch` repeats the backend's `Coverage.dispatcher` for the probe; change both or neither.
- An `AreaHandle` claims the pointer with `EagerGestureRecognizer`; without it the map's drag wins and pans under the point.
- A delivery carrier is checked against the company's country **on write**, so an edit resends
  `MODE`/`PROVIDER` only when they changed (a company that moved could not edit its old option otherwise).
